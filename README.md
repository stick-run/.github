## What Is Stick Run?

Stick Run is a classic side-scrolling endless runner featuring a small stickman who never stops moving forward. Your objective is simple: avoid obstacles, survive as long as possible, and beat your previous distance.

## Understanding the Stick Run Gameplay Experience

Stick Run may look like a simple stickman platformer, but its challenge comes from combining several movement decisions into a continuous sequence.

A crate might require a normal jump, while the next obstacle demands an immediate roll. A wide gap may require a double jump, but using that second jump too early could create a dangerous landing.

Players must think beyond the obstacle directly in front of them.

The most successful approach is to anticipate what comes next, preserve movement options, and avoid unnecessary actions.

### Crates and Box Jumps

Crates are more than obstacles. Their upper surfaces can also become useful platforms.

Landing safely on a crate allows you to jump again from its top, helping you navigate challenging sections.

However, hitting the side of a crate can end your run, so accurate positioning matters.

### Saw Blades and Dangerous Landings

Spinning saw blades are particularly hazardous because they can threaten both your current movement and your landing position.

Clearing one obstacle is not enough if your stickman lands directly on another.

Always consider where your jump will end.

### Glass Barriers and Rolling

Glass barriers encourage players to use the roll mechanic.

When a low barrier approaches, jumping may be the wrong reaction. Instead, prepare to roll through the available opening.

Learning to alternate smoothly between jumping and rolling is essential for longer runs.

### Gaps and Double Jumps

Gaps require careful timing.

Jumping too early or wasting your double jump can leave you unable to reach the next safe platform.

Try to reserve your second jump until you understand how much additional distance you need.

## Tips and Tricks to Get Better at Stick Run

Improving at Stick Run involves more than reacting quickly. Better anticipation and consistent timing can make a significant difference.

### Look Ahead of Your Character

Avoid focusing exclusively on the stickman.

Watch the upcoming obstacles so you can prepare your next action before the hazard reaches you.

### Save Your Double Jump

A double jump can rescue a difficult situation, but using it too early removes an important option.

Whenever possible, make your first jump deliberately and decide whether a second jump is necessary.

### Choose Safe Landing Areas

Before jumping, consider where your character will land.

Avoid landing directly in front of a crate, on a spinning saw blade, or too close to another dangerous obstacle.

### Learn When to Roll

Not every hazard should be jumped over.

If a low glass barrier approaches, use the Down Arrow to roll underneath it.

Choosing the correct movement matters more than pressing buttons quickly.

### Stay Calm During Difficult Sections

Rapid, unnecessary inputs can make a challenging section even harder.

Maintain a steady rhythm and avoid using movements without a clear purpose.

### Learn from Every Failed Attempt

After your run ends, consider why it happened.

Did you jump too late? Did you use your double jump too soon? Did you try to jump instead of roll?

Recognizing these mistakes can help improve your next attempt.
